Interesting facts about Jupiter

Learn more about Jupiter
  • Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system.
  • Jupiter has a strong magnetic field.
  • Jupiter has at least 79 moons.
  • Jupiter is known for its Great Red Spot, a giant storm that has been raging for centuries.
  1. Jupiter is a gas giant, meaning it is mostly made up of hydrogen and helium.
  2. Jupiter has a very fast rotation, completing one rotation in just under 10 hours.
  3. Jupiter's atmosphere is made up of clouds of ammonia and water vapor.
